# New eras brief (2026-10-12)

Three new periods, each a separate warp zone built by its own generator, following tools/gen/giantPlains.js
(route DEF with two forks, SECTIONS, NESTS x3, FEATURES, ruler DEN + FINAL stand, Cinematics EstShot markers,
arrival + return portals, routeData() for Routes/<Zone>.luau). Origins agreed with the Spine River session:

| Zone id | Name | Origin | Year | Look (Zone.luau) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RedCliffs | RED CLIFFS | (2100, 0, -900) | 75,000,000 BC | Gobi at golden dusk: warm orange sun low, long shadows, red/ochre haze, saturated |
| FrostForest | FROST FOREST | (2100, 0, -2600) | 125,000,000 BC | Snowy blue dawn: cool soft light, pale haze, gentle snowfall, a smoking volcano far off |
| DawnDesert | DAWN DESERT | (-2100, 0, -900) | 215,000,000 BC | Triassic sunrise: purple-pink sky, orange sun on the horizon, red dunes, heat shimmer |

Every era must look completely different from Fern Valley, Giant Plains and Spine River, and from each other.
Chunky studded toy-brick style, readable silhouettes, landmark lighting, mapqc clean (road clearance N = 0).

## Red Cliffs — "where the first dinosaur nests were dug up"
- Towering layered red sandstone cliffs (strata bands of red, orange, cream), eroded buttes and hoodoos, a huge
  natural rock arch the road drives under, sand-wave dunes, dry washes with scrubby saxaul bushes.
- A cliff face with a giant "Fighting Dinosaurs" fossil relief (a raptor locked with a Protoceratops) as a landmark.
- A hidden green river grove under the cliffs (conifers + ferns) = the ruler den.
- Route: Dune Road (under the arch) -> fork: Egg Ledges (cliff-side shelf road) | Dune Sea (rolling dunes, jump)
  -> Dry Wash -> fork: Hoodoo Maze | Runner Flats -> Homestretch.
- Sites: 1 Oviraptor "THIEF'S LEDGE" (cliff shelves), 2 Protoceratops "DUNE BURROWS", 3 Gallimimus "RUNNER FLATS".
- Ruler den: Therizinosaurus "SCYTHE GROVE" (river grove with tall trees it can rake). Weather: sandstorm wall,
  dust devils.

## Frost Forest — "cold forest lakes with winter snow and volcanic ash"
- Snow-laden dark pines, golden-leaved ginkgo trees on snow (signature colour contrast), frozen lakes with
  cracked blue ice, steaming hot springs, black volcanic ash ridges, a smoking volcano on the skyline.
- Route: Pine Road -> fork: Frozen Lake (ice road across the lake) | Ash Ridge (dark ash slope) -> Ginkgo Hollow
  -> fork: Hot Springs | Fallen Giants (huge fallen trunks) -> Homestretch.
- Sites: 1 Microraptor "GLIDER PINES", 2 Psittacosaurus "FERN HOLLOW", 3 Compy "LOG WARREN".
- Ruler den: Yutyrannus "WHITE LAKE" (frozen lake ringed by pines). Weather: snowfall, drifting ash flakes.

## Dawn Desert — "the first dinosaurs, still small"
- Red dunes and banded "painted" clay hills (purple, white, red stripes), giant petrified logs with glittering
  rainbow-quartz ends (Stone Forest), braided river oases with tall horsetails and ferns, red mesas.
- Route: Sunrise Road -> fork: Red Dunes | Painted Wash -> Stone Forest -> fork: Oasis Run | Mesa Rim -> Homestretch.
- Sites: 1 Coelophysis "OASIS PACK" (Pack x5), 2 Plateosaurus "TALL CYCADS", 3 Dilophosaurus "CREST ROCKS".
- Ruler den: Postosuchus "BONE WASH" (dry riverbed strewn with bones, mud it hides in). Weather: heat shimmer,
  dust devils.
