# Dino Expedition — artwork delivery

Created with the built-in image generation tool from the actual model renders, followed by deterministic text overlays and export. 28 first-round variants + 28 refinement images (two edits for each of two selected sources per ID), seven selected exports, a named-boss alternate, and 14 text-free runner-ups are saved.

**Fidelity limitation:** these are reference-guided generated illustrations, not exact rerenders of the 3D models. Small geometry differences remain across the set. The island scene in particular does not meet the brief's strict exact-map standard; it is included for review, not certified as a faithful gameplay image.

## Model drift and review notes

- **ICON:** Best getaway read: larger explorer face, recognizable Rookie Ranger, golden stepped egg. Fine rover hardware, tooth spacing and joint proportions are approximate; some foreground softness remains.
- **ICON_ALT:** Strong face/egg close-up and clean orange background. Rainbow is rendered as both surface color and glow; this is stronger than the described in-game rarity effect. No rover, so game-specific uniqueness scores lower.
- **T1:** Corrected olive raptor, three roof eggs, restored bedroll and thin dart. Raptor limb joints and launcher/mount geometry still differ from the original; roof rack cargo attachment is illustrative.
- **T2:** Clear stealing action, cream marked frill, brown studded body, three horns and waiting yellow-green rover. Minor horn angles, rover hardware and egg patterns vary; nest/environment are newly illustrated.
- **T3:** Six rarities in order, compact green hatchling, safari explorer and readable reveal. Baby head/body ratio and shell fracture are stylized; rarity surface tint is stronger than the described game effect.
- **T4:** Uncrowned green Tyrant King, corrected single launcher, two explorers and two orange darts. Launcher grip/barrel and dinosaur tooth/part spacing are approximations. Boss UI is illustrative, not matched to a provided UI screenshot.
- **T5:** Weakest exact-fidelity match. Gate is beyond the plaza again, but the island footprint, bridge routing, building spacing, central monument and elevated gate platform remain different from the real camp. Water and lighting are more glossy than the reference. Use as a marketing concept; it does not pass a strict exact-map requirement.

The first-round chase images with a redesigned bright-green raptor and boss images with merged launchers were excluded from final selection. The exact-map limitation on T5 remains unresolved after two edits per selected source.
